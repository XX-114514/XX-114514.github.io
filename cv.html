Jun 2025 – Present

Pan-Cancer Analysis of Tumor Microenvironment Cell-Communication Networks

  • Data integration: Integrated ~4.7 million single-cell transcriptomes across 30+ cancer types to characterize recurrent tumor-microenvironment cell states and multicellular communication programs.
  • Module discovery: Combined ligand–receptor inference and NMF-based module discovery to prioritize conserved immune- and stromal-associated regulatory niches across cancer cohorts.
  • Spatial validation: Mapped selected communication programs onto spatial transcriptomic datasets to evaluate in situ sender–receiver colocalization and spatial niche organization.

Jul – Aug 2026

EMT and Altered KRAS Inducibility in KRAS-Independent Pancreatic Cancer

  • Transcriptomic profiling: Compared parental AK38 and recurrent 772 PDAC cells across Dox ON/OFF conditions using DESeq2, GSEA, and pathway inference; identified EMT and E2F/G2M/MYC programs.
  • Cross-dataset analysis: Reanalyzed three public KRAS-withdrawal and relapse datasets to examine the reproducibility and specificity of EMT-associated transcription.
  • Proteomics & controls: Integrated RPPA, immunoblotting, genotyping, and a non-targeting CRISPR control to assess KRAS inducibility and prioritize DNMT1 and selective PDK1/AKT-related signals.

TP53RK Functional-Mode Analysis: Phosphorylation vs. KEOPS Dependence

  • Mutagenesis: Constructed phosphorylation-site and KEOPS-complex-site mutants of TP53RK and introduced them by lentiviral transduction.
  • Functional rescue: Degraded endogenous TP53RK with dTAG-V1 and used Incucyte to compare proliferation and cell-death rescue between mutant classes.
