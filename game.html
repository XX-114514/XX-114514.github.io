Interactive / Side project

Rogue CellA roguelike about immune evasion

You are a cancer cell inside a tissue microenvironment, playing out a fifteen-minute disease course in three acts — lung, then liver, then brain. Evade, absorb nutrients, mutate — and when you're ready, fight back on three fronts at once: split off decoys, turn immune cells against each other, and exploit the same checkpoints real tumors use to disappear from surveillance. The host doesn't hold out forever, but neither do you have to face it unchanged.
