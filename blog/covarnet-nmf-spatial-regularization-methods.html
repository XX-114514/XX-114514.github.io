Data and the role of each readout

Prototype data: 106 states, 755 single-cell samples and one 6,090-cell CosMx renal cancer field; rank 9, λ=0.1, l1_w=1e-3, l1_h=1e-4, CosMx view-weight multiplier 0.1. Thirty Frobenius and fifteen KL initializations share the same inputs.

Each readout has its own role: roughness is computed on the real graph used in training and measures the response of the graph lever (Figure 2); reconstruction error, regional held-out reconstruction, reference-basis matching and sparsity measure whether modules and fit are preserved (Figure 3); Moran's I after fixed-W projection is outside the training objective and evaluates spatial organization of tissue (Figure 4). Initializations and blocks are algorithmic and computational repetitions; biological inference uses patients or slides as units and is designed separately.

“Direction fraction” is the fraction with lower real-graph values for roughness and error metrics, and higher values for frozen_w_match. Relative changes in Figure 2a and Figure 3 come from the single-initialization prototype record (seed 17); paired comparisons across initializations are tabulated below.

Synthetic validation settings

30 states, rank 4, 200 single-cell samples, 600 spatial cells uniform on a 100×100 field; spatial kNN k=8, single-cell mutual kNN k=10 on CLR coordinates; λ=0.1 in both views, l1_w=1e-3, l1_h=1e-4, view weights 1/200 and 0.1/600, up to 300 iterations, 8 seeds. In the unorganized scenario spatial activities are drawn independently per cell from Gamma(1,1); in the hotspot scenario each module is a Gaussian hotspot plus noise. Moran's I is computed on H from fixed-W Frobenius projection without graph terms and averaged over modules. Results: organized 0.908, unorganized -0.004; organized scenario across four training configurations 0.907–0.908. Results and parameters. On the same synthetic data, paired training-graph roughness comparisons are 8/8 in both scenarios, which is why section 05 uses a readout separate from the training objective.

Model and implementation interfaces

PartImplementation
CoVarNet baselineR nsNMF, X≈WSH; correlation filters define the subsequent module network.
Project_v2 discoverysklearn NMF with the default Frobenius loss, listed separately from the R nsNMF configuration.
Project_v2 recoveryFixed W with H-only KL multiplicative updates.
SpaCoVarNet-GSShared W, separate H, Frobenius/generalized KL, graph terms, L1 and optional dissimilarity; reported δ=0.
Feature interfaceIdentifiers and order align upstream; the solver checks dimensions, finite nonnegative values and graph structure.
Memory representationSparse local adjacency and graph arithmetic; dense view arrays, factors and WH.

Generalized KL is used as a reconstruction criterion for nonnegative compositions. xlogy handles zero terms and epsilon protects denominators. Fixed-λ comparisons evaluate directional consistency under the stated settings. Factor scale affects penalties; column normalization supports factor comparisons and training constraints retain their objective-specific interpretation.

Rewiring preserves unweighted node degrees and the global edge-weight multiset; node strengths can change. State graphs and anchors belong to the design roadmap, with the implemented scope specified above.

sklearn NMF API · NMF reference

v1 estimator specification: W columns are constrained to sum to one so that the strength of λ and β is independent of factor scale; inputs carry an observation mask and parent-count offsets; the spatial graph uses the normalized Laplacian D^{-1/2} L D^{-1/2}.

Source-weight sidebar

The October 2026 sensitivity retains nsNMF and evaluates how source sample allocation influences module shape. For n_s units in source s, N total units and S sources, w_j=N/(S n_s) gives equal total source objective weight N/S.

D_KL(w_j X_j || w_j Y_j) = w_j D_KL(X_j || Y_j) X′_j = w_j X_j H_j = H′_j / w_j

H is restored after fitting; nsNMF reconstruction comparisons use the effective basis WS with one-to-one component matching. Source contribution and spatial adjacency have separately defined evaluation targets.
