Analysis scope and evaluation roles

The historical experiment uses 106 states, 755 samples, one 6,090-cell CosMx field and rank 9. Thirty Frobenius and fifteen KL initializations reuse the same inputs. Activity roughness, frozen-basis matching and regional reconstruction address neighborhood response, reference correspondence and dictionary explanation respectively.

Roughness is evaluated on the common real graph and targets the representation property encouraged by graph regularization. Initializations are algorithmic repetitions; blocks are computational holdouts within one field. Patient or slide units support the corresponding biological inference.

The aggregate evidence JSON retains the full comparison family. Reported P values use the historical scripts' nominal convention.

Both regional experiments train with Frobenius. Each coordinate partition contains eight blocks, with held-out observations excluded before rebuilding training graphs. W is fixed and held-out X is used to estimate H; MSE and mean KL measure reconstruction. The two partitions reuse the same field and training folds overlap.

Real-graph mean MSE is lower than for both null controls in each partition, while block-level directions vary. Mean MSE differences against no graph are positive. These results describe a fitting tradeoff between activity organization and dictionary reconstruction; a stable held-out reconstruction benefit was not established.

The no_graph configuration disables both sc and spatial penalties; the other three configurations retain the sc graph. Real versus no graph therefore changes the combined graph constraints, while real versus null graphs compares spatial structure conditional on the shared sc graph.

Feature interfaceIdentifiers and order align upstream; the solver checks dimensions, finite nonnegative values and graph structure.

Generalized KL is used as a reconstruction criterion for nonnegative compositions. xlogy handles zero terms and epsilon protects denominators. Fixed-λ comparisons evaluate directional consistency under the stated settings. Factor scale affects penalties; column normalization supports factor comparisons and training constraints retain their objective-specific interpretation.

Rewiring preserves unweighted node degrees and the global edge-weight multiset; node strengths can change. State graphs and anchors belong to the design roadmap, with the implemented scope specified above.

Source-weight sidebar

The October 2026 sensitivity retains nsNMF and evaluates how source sample allocation influences module shape. For n_s units in source s, N total units and S sources, w_j=N/(S n_s) gives equal total source objective weight N/S.

D_KL(w_j X_j || w_j Y_j) = w_j D_KL(X_j || Y_j) X′_j = w_j X_j H_j = H′_j / w_j

H is restored after fitting; nsNMF reconstruction comparisons use the effective basis WS with one-to-one component matching. Source contribution and spatial adjacency have separately defined evaluation targets.
