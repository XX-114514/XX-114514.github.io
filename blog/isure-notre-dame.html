Altered Inducibility in KRAS-Independent Pancreatic CancerFrom EMT-Factor Perturbation to DNMT1 and PI3K Hypotheses

This summer project examined why EMT-factor perturbation strengthened doxycycline-induced KRAS protein responses in the recurrent 772 model without producing a clear mesenchymal-to-epithelial transition.

Author
Xin Xiong
Program
iSURE, Notre Dame
Date
Summer 2026
Status
Research summary

Research overview

PDAC / KRAS / EMT

Question and approach

I compared parental AK38 cells with the KRAS-independent recurrent derivative 772 using transcriptomic comparisons, genotyping, non-targeting controls, immunoblotting, RPPA, and three public mouse PDAC datasets.

The analysis separated early KRAS withdrawal, residual survival, stable escape, and Dox re-exposure so that EMT-associated transcription could be interpreted against the correct biological reference.

Main interpretation

  • EMT-associated transcription recurred across withdrawal and relapse models.
  • 772 recovered proliferative programs while retaining a transcriptional Dox response.
  • Elevated DNMT1 and selective PDK1/AKT-substrate changes prioritized two testable hypotheses.
  • Methylation measurements and functional perturbations remain necessary for causal claims.
